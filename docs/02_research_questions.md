differentially affect working-memory performance?

- Calibrated reaction time

### Conditions
- Positive
- Negative
- Neutral

### Cognitive load

- 2-back
- 3-back
- 4-back


## RQ2 — Negative Emotion

Does negative emotion impair working-memory performance
relative to the neutral condition?

### Secondary question

Is the effect characterized by:

- lower accuracy?
- slower reaction time?
- faster but less accurate responses?
- both lower accuracy and slower responses?
