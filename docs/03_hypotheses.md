# Hypotheses

## H1 — Emotional Condition

Positive, negative, and neutral emotional conditions
differentially affect working-memory performance.

## H2 — Negative Emotion

Negative emotion leads to poorer working-memory performance
compared with the neutral condition.

### H2a
Negative emotion decreases accuracy.

### H2b
Negative emotion affects calibrated reaction time.

## H3 — Positive Emotion

Positive emotion improves working-memory performance
relative to negative and neutral conditions.

### H3a
Positive emotion increases accuracy.

### H3b
Positive emotion improves reaction-time performance.

## H4 — Cognitive Load Moderation

The effect of emotional condition on working-memory
performance varies according to cognitive load.

## H5 — Emotion Manipulation

Emotion induction produces immediate changes in SAM valence.

### H5a
Positive induction increases valence.

### H5b
Negative induction decreases valence.

## H6 — n-back Effect on Mood

Working-memory task execution affects immediate mood valence.
